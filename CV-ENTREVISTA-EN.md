## Respuestas de entrevista (60–90 segundos cada una)

### 1. "Tell me about yourself"

> "I'm a customer support specialist with three years of experience handling phone, chat and email for [type of customers]. I'm also a trained electrician, which taught me to troubleshoot systematically and explain technical problems in simple terms. I'm fully bilingual in Spanish and English, and I'm looking for a remote role where I can use both — supporting customers and solving problems, not just following a script."

### 2. "Tell me about a difficult customer" (método STAR)

> "**Situation:** A customer called furious because his service had been down for two days and he'd already been transferred three times.
> **Task:** My job was to resolve it without escalating, and keep him as a customer.
> **Action:** First I let him explain everything without interrupting, then I summarized his problem back to him so he knew I understood. I checked the account, found the billing block causing the outage, fixed it while he was on the line, and gave him a specific time when service would return.
> **Result:** He stayed with us and gave a positive review. I now use the same approach — listen, confirm, fix, follow up — and it works with most angry customers."

### 3. "Why do you want to work remotely?"

> "I've been working with distributed teams and digital tools for years, and remote work is where I'm most productive — no commute, fewer interruptions, and I can focus on the customer. I also know it requires discipline: I keep a fixed schedule, a dedicated workspace and clear communication with my team. I'm set up to work on US time zones."

### 4. "Why should we hire you?"

> "Three reasons. First, I have three years of real customer support experience, so I can start taking contacts in the first week. Second, my electrical background means I can handle technical products faster than a generalist — I understand how things work, not just what the manual says. Third, I'm fully bilingual and comfortable with US customers, which means one agent covers both markets."

### 5. "What are your salary expectations?"

> "I'm flexible and more interested in the role and growth. Based on similar remote support roles, I'd expect something in the range of [USD X–Y] per hour — but I'd like to understand the full package and the schedule first."

(Nunca digas una cifra sola: da un rango y devuelve la pregunta.)

## Preguntas que TÚ debes hacer

1. "What does success look like in the first 90 days?"
2. "What are the working hours and time zone for this role?"
3. "Which tools does the team use day to day?"
4. "How is performance measured — CSAT, resolution time, ticket volume?"
5. "Is this a contractor or employee position, and how are payments made?"

## Práctica

- Grábate en video respondiendo las 5. Míralo una vez y repite.
- No memorices palabra por palabra: memoriza las 3 ideas de cada respuesta.
- Ten a mano: CV en PDF, libreta con notas, agua, y el link de la vacante abierto.
- Si no entiendes una pregunta: "Sorry, could you repeat that?" — es normal y no resta.
